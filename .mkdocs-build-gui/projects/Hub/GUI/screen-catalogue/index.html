    Screen catalogue

    This catalogue describes the visible responsibilities of the GUI. A screen may be composed of a route, tabs, panels, dialogs, grids, and related-record components rather than a single page.

    Common screen families

    Family User experience
    Search/list Enter criteria, run a paged query, inspect a grid/table, open a preview or detail view
    Add/create Select a creation context, enter a form, validate, save, and continue to the new record
    Edit Load existing data and lookups, change fields, validate, save, and refresh the record
    View/profile Show summary and detail information, then navigate related tabs and panels
    Relationship panel Query children using the parent identifier and create or update associations
    Preview panel Show a lightweight record view without leaving the current list or parent page
    Modal/dialog Complete a focused command such as confirmation, upload, selection, merge, or validation
    Publication/marketing Manage publication state, images, brochures, visibility, and marketing actions
    Administration Work with role-protected configuration lists and edit dialogs

    Domain areas

    Dashboard

    The dashboard is a configurable work surface. It contains widgets for activities, requirements, tenancies, callbacks, CDD, marketing, instructions, web enquiries, property visits, notifications, business generation, market overview, and other operational areas. Widgets provide counters, lists, calendar views, and drill-down actions. SignalR-backed notifications can update the notification state.

    Contacts

    Contact screens support search, create, edit, and profile views. A contact profile brings together personal details, addresses, phones and emails, activities, requirements, referrals, interests, negotiators, consent, notes, communications, and related companies.

    Companies

    Company screens support search, create, edit, and profile views. Company profiles expose contacts, activities, requirements, instructions, ownership, tenancies, interests, addresses, notes, and related property information.

    Properties and property hierarchy

    Property screens cover search, create/edit, property checks, profile views, ownership, activities, attachments, publication, marketing, and related assets. Development, building, estate, phase, and portfolio areas provide hierarchical or grouped property views:

    • developments contain buildings and units;
    • buildings expose units, ownership, activities, attachments, and property breakdown;
    • estates and phases expose related property and activity management;
    • portfolios coordinate properties and tenancies at portfolio level.

    Activities

    Activity screens cover search, create/edit, preview, parties, terms and conditions, publication, marketing assets, offers, requirements, matches, viewings, appointments, referrals, and state changes. The edit experience varies by service line and creation context, including activity creation from properties, buildings, developments, estates, phases, portfolios, and other records.

    Requirements and matching

    Requirement screens support search, saved searches, create/edit, applicant management, matching, viewing tours, progression, and instruction linkage. Matching screens connect requirements to activities and allow users to progress or arrange viewings.

    Offers

    Offer screens support search, create/edit, preview, acceptance, rejection, withdrawal, reservation-specific status handling, and linkage to an activity. Offer forms calculate and validate price, area-based amounts, annual amounts, and lease dates where applicable.

    Tenancies

    Tenancy screens support search, view/edit, progression, renewal, termination, deposit protection, paperwork upload, email upload, appointments, compliance, and related parties. Tenancy workflows can be initiated from properties, estates, activities, and other contexts.

    Instructions, pitches, and referrals

    These areas provide search, create/edit, view, related activity, task, document, and progression behavior. Referral and instruction creation can be entered from related requirements, activities, or referrals.

    Marketing lists

    Marketing-list screens support search, create/edit, contact upload, contact editing, merge, export, and related marketing communication behavior. Merge is restricted to the Marketeer role.

    Reports

    The report area lets the user select a report, provide criteria, generate the result, and download or view the output. Domain menus can open this area with a preselected report group.

    Administration and configuration

    Administration provides tabs and dialogs for users, departments, Dotmailer, configurations, templates, showovers, marketing communication topics, subscriptions, LIA rules, and portal document visibility. Configuration provides system configuration and translations and is limited to system administrators.

    Shared content available across domains

    Most major records can expose:

    • activities and current activities;
    • notes and journal/history;
    • attachments and document previews;
    • addresses, contacts, ownership, and parties;
    • appointments, viewings, emails, text messages, and notifications;
    • maps and location information;
    • status, alerts, consent, CDD, and compliance information;
    • related-record search and navigation.

    Evidence

    The catalogue is based on the feature folders under src/app/, the route modules, component templates, and the existing Angular GUI analysis.
