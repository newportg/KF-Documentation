    Company pages

    Routes and entry points

    • /company — search.
    • /company/check — duplicate check before creation.
    • Company add/edit — create or update.
    • Company view — summary and related records.

    The company menu exposes Search and Create. Company records are also opened from contact, property, activity, requirement, ownership, and tenancy flows.

    Search page

    Filters

    • company name and general search;
    • category;
    • client-care status;
    • company type;
    • funds versus companies;
    • company hierarchy.

    Results

    The result table can display client, holding, KF-verified, child, and fund markers. Empty results show a no-items state and search refinement actions.

    Add/edit page

    Sections

    • alternative company information;
    • basic information;
    • holding company;
    • relationship manager;
    • negotiators;
    • CDD validity where applicable;
    • additional information;
    • sticky Cancel and Save footer.

    Create and edit use the same domain structure, with title and mode-specific controls. Duplicate checking precedes creation.

    View page

    Summary and profile

    • company summary;
    • owning/holding company;
    • funds and children;
    • negotiators and CDD validity;
    • basic and additional information;
    • invoice addresses;
    • portal information;
    • notes and activity/history.

    Related lists

    • contacts;
    • activities;
    • requirements;
    • instructions;
    • pitches;
    • ownerships and tenancies;
    • interests;
    • property-management suppliers.

    Related lists expose filters, clear-all actions, item counts, empty states, and domain-specific tables. Company activities include title, type, status, price/rent, negotiator, date, and company columns. Company contacts include name, company, branch, job title, phone, email, negotiator, portal, and address information.

    Validation and navigation

    • Company add/edit routes use dirty-navigation protection.
    • Company search and view routes use query/parameter-sensitive route reuse.
    • Company relationship changes can open confirmation or validation panels.
